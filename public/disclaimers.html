Details & disclaimers

About this prototype

Back Off My Data is a concept for feedback. It does not currently accept payment, create accounts, send deletion requests, follow up, or place credit freezes. The $79.99 annual price and daily follow-up are proposed, not available services.

Deletion requests

Any future service would depend on authorization, verification, applicable law, company policies, and available technical channels. Request frequency and results may vary. Deletion is not guaranteed, and no service can promise that personal data will never be collected again.

Free California option

California residents can use the state's free Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP). A DROP request remains active without daily resubmission; data brokers process requests at least every 45 days. Some information and businesses may be exempt. See how DROP works.

Credit freezes

Credit freezes are free when requested directly from each of the three nationwide credit bureaus. Learn more from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.
